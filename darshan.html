🕉️ Pilgrim Guidelines

Sacred Rules & Darshan Etiquette

🪔

Aarti Participation

Devotees are welcome to participate in Morning Aarti (7:30 AM) and Sandhya Aarti (7:00 PM). Please arrive 15 minutes prior.

👞

Footwear Stand

Please deposit footwear at the designated shoe stand near the temple entrance before entering the sacred sanctum.

🥥

Offerings & Prasad

Fresh flowers, chunri, coconut, and satvik sweets can be offered to Maa. Sacred Prasad is distributed after every Aarti.
