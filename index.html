About Adi Shakti Maa Vaishno Bhavtarni Mandir

Situated in the sacred town of Titwala, Maharashtra, Adi Shakti Maa Vaishno Bhavtarni Mandir is a sanctified shrine dedicated to the Divine Mother. Devotees visit this sacred abode to seek Maa's eternal blessings, strength, and inner spiritual peace.

Inspired by the eternal power of Adi Shakti, the Mandir enshrines the holy Tridevi (Maa Mahakali, Maa Mahalakshmi, Maa Mahasaraswati) alongside the sacred Charan Paduka, uniting thousands of devotees through daily worship, Vedic rituals, and festival celebrations.
